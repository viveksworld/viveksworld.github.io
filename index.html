Three things, at enterprise scale.

01

Build platforms

Cloud, data, and AI platforms that engineering teams can adopt, scale, and operate with confidence — built once as shared capabilities, not as one-off solutions.

02 Impact

Problems, what I led, and what changed.

Applying AI reasoning, deterministic automation, and governance controls to the repeatable work of running cloud infrastructure.

Problem

Infrastructure engineers spend significant time fulfilling repeatable operational requests — capacity that could go to higher-value platform work.

What I led

A reusable AI-enabled automation platform spanning cloud, Kubernetes, and data services, with validation, approvals, and traceability built in.

Outcome

More engineering capacity, faster request fulfillment, and quicker time-to-market for application teams — adopted progressively across environments.

High availability and disaster recovery for a large-scale data platform.

Problem

A business-critical data platform spanning many Azure services needed recovery that worked in practice, not just on paper.

What I led

Designed high availability and disaster recovery strategy across Kubernetes, Databricks, Snowflake, and Azure services; recovery automation; and DR exercises.

Outcome

Proven recovery capability and operational readiness, with support teams trained and equipped through SOPs.

Infrastructure foundations for a multi-application enterprise data platform.

Problem

Multiple application teams needed a governed, secure, and scalable data platform rather than isolated environments.

What I led

Platform design covering networking, identity, access control, capacity, automation, and supportability.

Outcome

A shared foundation teams can onboard to, with consistent security and operational standards, so every application gets the same guardrails from day one.

Bringing generative AI and agent platforms into a regulated enterprise — safely.

Problem

Emerging AI services move faster than enterprise identity, network, and governance patterns are typically ready for.

What I led

Platform enablement and engineering readiness for AI services — identity, access, private networking, observability, and governance alignment.

Outcome

A path for application teams to adopt AI capabilities on a secure, supportable foundation.

03 How I think

Principles I keep coming back to.

01

Build platforms, not one-off solutions.

Solve a problem once, then make it a capability others can consume without needing me in the room.

02

Automate repetition. Preserve judgment.

AI and automation should return engineering capacity — not remove the human decisions that genuinely matter.

03

Design for failure.

Resiliency, recovery, and operational readiness are architecture decisions, not afterthoughts.

04

Governance should enable adoption.

Identity, controls, and observability are what make emerging technology safe enough to scale.

05

Learn. Unlearn. Relearn.

Technologies change every few years. The ability to understand new systems deeply and quickly is what lasts.
